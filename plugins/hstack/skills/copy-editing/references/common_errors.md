## 1. Word Choice Errors

### Commonly Confused Words

#### Accept vs. Except
- **Accept**: to receive (verb) - "I accept your apology"
- **Except**: excluding (preposition) - "Everyone except John attended"

#### Affect vs. Effect
- **Affect**: to influence (verb) - "The weather affects my mood"
- **Effect**: result (noun) - "The effect was immediate"
- **Effect**: to cause (verb, less common) - "To effect change"

#### Allusion vs. Illusion
- **Allusion**: indirect reference - "Her speech contained an allusion to Shakespeare"
- **Illusion**: false perception - "The magician created an illusion"

#### Assure vs. Ensure vs. Insure
- **Assure**: to promise/convince someone - "I assure you it's safe"
- **Ensure**: to make certain - "Please ensure the door is locked"
- **Insure**: to provide insurance - "We need to insure the package"

#### Complement vs. Compliment
- **Complement**: to complete/enhance - "The wine complements the meal"
- **Compliment**: praise - "She gave me a compliment"

#### Discreet vs. Discrete
- **Discreet**: careful, prudent - "Please be discreet about this matter"
- **Discrete**: separate, distinct - "The report has three discrete sections"

#### Farther vs. Further
- **Farther**: physical distance - "The store is farther than I thought"
- **Further**: metaphorical distance/additionally - "Further discussion is needed"

#### Imply vs. Infer
- **Imply**: to suggest indirectly (speaker/writer) - "She implied I was late"
- **Infer**: to deduce (listener/reader) - "I inferred from her tone"

#### Its vs. It's
- **Its**: possessive - "The dog wagged its tail"
- **It's**: contraction of "it is" or "it has" - "It's been raining"

#### Lose vs. Loose
- **Lose**: to misplace (verb) - "Don't lose your keys"
- **Loose**: not tight (adjective) - "The screw is loose"

#### Principal vs. Principle
- **Principal**: main/head person (noun/adjective) - "The principal reason"
- **Principle**: rule or belief - "It's a matter of principle"

#### Than vs. Then
- **Than**: comparison - "Taller than me"
- **Then**: time sequence - "First this, then that"

#### Their vs. There vs. They're
- **Their**: possessive - "Their house"
- **There**: location - "Over there"
- **They're**: contraction of "they are" - "They're coming"

#### Your vs. You're
- **Your**: possessive - "Your book"
- **You're**: contraction of "you are" - "You're welcome"

## 2. Grammar Errors

### Subject-Verb Agreement

#### Error: Collective Noun Confusion
- **Wrong**: "The team are winning"
- **Right**: "The team is winning" (American English)

#### Error: Compound Subjects
- **Wrong**: "John and Mary is here"
- **Right**: "John and Mary are here"

#### Error: Indefinite Pronouns
- **Wrong**: "Everyone have their books"
- **Right**: "Everyone has their books" or "Everyone has his or her books"

### Pronoun Errors

#### Error: Pronoun Case
- **Wrong**: "Between you and I"
- **Right**: "Between you and me"

#### Error: Who vs. Whom
- **Wrong**: "Who did you give it to?"
- **Right**: "Whom did you give it to?" or "To whom did you give it?"

#### Error: Unclear Antecedent
- **Wrong**: "John told Mark that he was promoted" (who was promoted?)
- **Right**: "John told Mark, 'You were promoted'" or "John told Mark, 'I was promoted'"

### Verb Tense Errors

#### Error: Tense Shifting
- **Wrong**: "She walked to the store and buys milk"
- **Right**: "She walked to the store and bought milk"

#### Error: Perfect Tense Misuse
- **Wrong**: "I have went to the store"
- **Right**: "I have gone to the store"

### Modifier Errors

#### Error: Dangling Modifier
- **Wrong**: "Walking down the street, the building was impressive"
- **Right**: "Walking down the street, I found the building impressive"

#### Error: Misplaced Modifier
- **Wrong**: "She only ate two cookies" (if she did more than just eat them)
- **Right**: "She ate only two cookies"

## 3. Punctuation Errors

### Comma Errors

#### Error: Comma Splice
- **Wrong**: "It's late, we should go"
- **Right**: "It's late. We should go" or "It's late; we should go" or "It's late, so we should go"

#### Error: Missing Oxford Comma
- **Ambiguous**: "I love my parents, Lady Gaga and Humpty Dumpty"
- **Clear**: "I love my parents, Lady Gaga, and Humpty Dumpty"

#### Error: Unnecessary Comma
- **Wrong**: "The man, who stole my bike, was arrested" (if there's only one man)
- **Right**: "The man who stole my bike was arrested"

### Apostrophe Errors

#### Error: Its/It's Confusion
- **Wrong**: "The company lost it's license"
- **Right**: "The company lost its license"

#### Error: Possessive Plurals
- **Wrong**: "The childrens' toys"
- **Right**: "The children's toys"

#### Error: Plural vs. Possessive
- **Wrong**: "We sell pizza's"
- **Right**: "We sell pizzas"

### Semicolon Errors

#### Error: Using with Dependent Clause
- **Wrong**: "I went to the store; because we needed milk"
- **Right**: "I went to the store because we needed milk"

## 4. Style Errors

### Passive Voice Overuse
- **Weak**: "The report was written by the committee"
- **Strong**: "The committee wrote the report"

### Nominalizations
- **Weak**: "We made a decision to proceed"
- **Strong**: "We decided to proceed"

### Wordy Phrases

| Wordy | Concise |
|--------|---------|
| At this point in time | Now |
| Due to the fact that | Because |
| In order to | To |
| In the event that | If |
| For the purpose of | To/For |
| Has the ability to | Can |
| In close proximity to | Near |
| Make a decision | Decide |
| Conduct an investigation | Investigate |

- Paradigm shift

- Game-changer

## 5. Consistency Errors

### Number Format Inconsistency
- **Wrong**: "We interviewed 12 managers and twenty employees"
- **Right**: "We interviewed 12 managers and 20 employees"

### Date Format Inconsistency
- **Wrong**: "From January 15, 2024 to 02/20/24"
- **Right**: "From January 15, 2024 to February 20, 2024"

### Serial Comma Inconsistency
- **Wrong**: "Apples, oranges and bananas in one list, then apples, oranges, and grapes in another"
- **Right**: Use consistent approach throughout document

### Hyphenation Inconsistency
- **Wrong**: "Multi-tasking in one paragraph, multitasking in another"
- **Right**: Choose one form and use consistently

### Capitalization Inconsistency
- **Wrong**: "The President in one sentence, the president in another (same person)"
- **Right**: Choose one style and maintain throughout

## 6. Technical Writing Errors

### Unclear Antecedents in Instructions
- **Wrong**: "Click the button. It will open the menu."
- **Right**: "Click the Settings button. The Settings menu will open."

### Missing Steps
- **Wrong**: "1. Open the file. 2. Save the document."
- **Right**: "1. Open the file. 2. Make your changes. 3. Save the document."

### Inconsistent Terminology
- **Wrong**: Using "click," "select," and "choose" interchangeably
- **Right**: Pick one term and use consistently

### Ambiguous Pronouns in Documentation
- **Wrong**: "The system sends the user their data"
- **Right**: "The system sends users their data" or "The system sends each user their data"

4. Unclear pronoun references

### Low Priority (Consider Context)

2. Regional variations

4. Author's voice preservation
