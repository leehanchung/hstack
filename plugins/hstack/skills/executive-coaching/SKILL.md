# Executive Coaching

Use for coaching technical leaders through conflict, growth, burnout, cofounder dynamics, and CTO transition planning.

## Default Stance

Coach when the person has context and can solve the problem. Advise when urgent, when they lack context, or when you have a specific answer. Ask: "Do you want advice, or space to think it through?"

## Core Moves

Listen for content, emotion, and context. Use GROW questions: goal, reality, options, way forward. Keep ownership with the coachee and end with a concrete next step.

## Difficult Conversations

Use NVC for conflict: observation, feeling, need, request. Stay factual, avoid blame, and ask how the person may be contributing to the pattern.

## Burnout and Role Design

Look for chronic depletion, not just workload. Track energizing and draining work, name strengths, and redesign toward more high-energy work.
