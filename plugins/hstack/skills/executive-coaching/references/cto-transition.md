# CTO Transition Coaching Framework

Complete framework for Senior Director → CTO transition in large enterprises (10k+ employees).

## Core Coaching Process

### Phase 1: Assessment & Goal Setting (Weeks 1-2)

#### Initial Data Gathering
- **360-degree feedback**: Collect from peers, reports, and senior leadership
- **Current scope analysis**: Document current responsibilities vs CTO expectations
- **Enterprise readiness**: Assess understanding of 10k+ employee dynamics
- **Technical vision**: Evaluate ability to set org-wide technology strategy

Key questions to explore:
- How do you currently influence beyond your 25-person team?
- What enterprise-wide initiatives have you led or contributed to?
- How comfortable are you presenting to C-suite and board members?
- What's your experience with technology budget planning at scale ($50M+)?

#### Goal Setting Framework
Create SMART goals across three horizons:
- **Immediate (30 days)**: Visibility and credibility building
- **Short-term (90 days)**: Strategic project leadership
- **Long-term (6-12 months)**: CTO readiness demonstrations

See `goal-templates.md` for enterprise-specific examples.

### Phase 2: Competency Development (Ongoing)

#### 1. Strategic Thinking (from team to enterprise)
- Shift from managing 25 engineers to influencing 500+ technologists
- Move from project delivery to portfolio strategy
- Develop technology vision aligned with business strategy

**Action items:**
- Shadow current CTO/VP Engineering in strategy sessions
- Lead cross-functional initiative with 3+ departments
- Present quarterly technology strategy to senior leadership

#### 2. Executive Presence
- Communication style for C-suite and board interactions
- Managing up and sideways (peer CTOs, CFO, CEO relationships)
- Public speaking and external representation

**Development approach:**
- Record and review presentation style
- Practice executive briefings (3-minute rule for complex topics)
- Media training for external representation

#### 3. Business Acumen Enhancement
- P&L responsibility understanding
- ROI articulation for technology investments
- Market dynamics and competitive analysis

**Key activities:**
- Partner with CFO on technology budget planning
- Lead build vs. buy analysis for major platform
- Present technology investment case to executive committee

#### 4. Organizational Leadership at Scale
Current: 25 direct/indirect reports → Target: 500+ organization
- Building leadership teams and succession planning
- Culture and change management at enterprise scale
- Managing through multiple layers of leadership

### Phase 3: Stakeholder Management

#### Enterprise Stakeholder Map
Critical relationships for CTO success:
1. **CEO**: Strategic alignment, vision translation
2. **CFO**: Budget partnership, ROI demonstration
3. **Other C-suite**: Cross-functional collaboration
4. **Board members**: Technology education, risk management
5. **External partners**: Vendor management, industry presence

Use `stakeholder-strategies.md` for engagement tactics.

#### Building Coalition Support
- Identify 5 key sponsors for CTO advancement
- Develop peer relationships with other CTOs/VPs
- Create visibility through enterprise-wide initiatives

### Phase 4: Practical Application

#### 90-Day CTO Readiness Projects
Select 2-3 from these enterprise-appropriate initiatives:
1. Lead digital transformation workstream affecting 1000+ employees
2. Present technology strategy at board meeting or committee
3. Manage crisis/incident with enterprise-wide impact
4. Drive $10M+ technology investment decision
5. Represent company at industry conference or with major partner

#### Weekly Coaching Sessions Structure
1. **Check-in** (10 min): Progress on action items
2. **Skill development** (30 min): Focus on specific competency
3. **Case study** (15 min): Real scenario problem-solving
4. **Planning** (5 min): Next week's objectives

### Phase 5: Measurement & Feedback

#### Success Metrics
Track progress monthly:
- Stakeholder feedback scores (target: 4.5/5.0)
- Strategic initiative leadership (target: 2-3 enterprise-wide)
- Executive visibility events (target: 1-2 per month)
- Direct influence scope (target: expand from 25 to 100+ in 6 months)

#### Feedback Loops
- Monthly 1:1 with current manager/sponsor
- Quarterly 360 review with expanded stakeholder group
- Continuous self-assessment against CTO competency model

## Critical Success Factors

### For Large Enterprise CTO Role
1. **Systems thinking**: Understand interdependencies across 10k+ organization
2. **Political navigation**: Manage competing priorities and stakeholder interests
3. **Risk management**: Balance innovation with enterprise stability
4. **Communication scale**: From team meetings to town halls (25 → 2000+ people)
5. **Decision velocity**: Make high-stakes decisions with incomplete information

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- Staying too tactical (continuing to code or architect)
- Underestimating stakeholder management importance
- Focusing only on technology without business connection
- Not building peer relationships early enough
- Waiting for permission instead of taking initiative

## Coaching Approach

### Principles
1. **Partnership**: Executive owns their development with coach as guide
2. **Business focus**: All development tied to enterprise value creation
3. **Systems perspective**: Consider organizational impact of changes
4. **Results orientation**: Measurable progress toward CTO readiness
5. **Confidentiality**: Safe space for vulnerability and growth

### Session Cadence
- **Intensive phase** (Month 1): 2x weekly, 60 minutes
- **Development phase** (Months 2-6): Weekly, 60 minutes
- **Sustaining phase** (Months 7-12): Bi-weekly, 60 minutes
- **On-demand**: Critical situation coaching as needed

### Coaching Methods
- **Direct observation**: Shadow in leadership meetings
- **Role play**: Practice board presentations and difficult conversations
- **Case analysis**: Review CTO-level decisions and scenarios
- **Peer learning**: Connect with other CTOs and senior leaders
- **Action learning**: Real projects with coaching support

## When to Engage Specialist Support

Consider additional specialized coaching for:
- **Financial acumen**: If P&L experience is limited
- **Public speaking**: For conference keynotes and media
- **Board readiness**: Specific board interaction preparation
- **Industry expertise**: Domain-specific technology trends
