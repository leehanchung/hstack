# ML Paper Writing

Use for ML/AI research papers targeting venues such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, AAAI, and COLM. Be proactive when the repo, results, and contribution are clear.

## Non-Negotiable Citation Rule

Never invent citations or BibTeX. Search, verify, fetch BibTeX from DOI when possible, and mark unresolved references as placeholders.

## Writing Workflow

1. Inspect repo, README, results, figures, `.bib`, and experiment outputs.
2. Compress the contribution into 1-3 claims and supporting experiments.
3. Build the narrative: what, why, so what.
4. Draft or revise the necessary sections.
5. Red-team claim strength against evidence.
6. Check venue formatting, anonymity, page limits, fonts, limitations, and reproducibility.

## Quality Bar

The introduction must make the contribution legible before methods. Every experiment states the claim it tests. Baselines must be fair. Figures should be self-contained, readable, and colorblind-safe. Claim strength must match evidence.

## References

Load citation, structure, evidence, writing, style, formatting, checklist, abstract, or reviewer references only when the current paper task needs them.
