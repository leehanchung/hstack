# CTO Development Goal Templates

## 30-60-90 Day Goals Framework

### First 30 Days: Foundation & Visibility

#### Strategic Leadership Goals
- **Goal**: Articulate technology vision to 3 different audiences
  - **Action**: Present to team (technical depth), peers (collaboration), executives (business value)
  - **Success Metric**: Positive feedback from each audience level
  - **Timeline**: Week 2, 3, 4

- **Goal**: Map current technology portfolio to business strategy
  - **Action**: Create visual alignment of tech initiatives to company OKRs
  - **Success Metric**: CEO/exec team validates alignment accuracy
  - **Timeline**: Complete by day 30

#### Stakeholder Relationship Goals
- **Goal**: Build relationships with C-suite peers
  - **Action**: Schedule 1:1s with each C-suite member
  - **Success Metric**: Understand top 3 priorities for each executive
  - **Timeline**: 2-3 meetings per week

- **Goal**: Establish technology leadership team cadence
  - **Action**: Implement weekly leadership team meetings with clear agenda
  - **Success Metric**: Consistent attendance and engagement
  - **Timeline**: Start week 1

### Days 31-60: Demonstrate Leadership

#### Strategic Initiative Goals
- **Goal**: Lead one enterprise-wide initiative
  - **Action**: Volunteer for or propose cross-functional project
  - **Success Metric**: Executive sponsor endorsement
  - **Example**: Digital transformation workstream, platform modernization

- **Goal**: Present to executive committee
  - **Action**: Prepare and deliver strategic technology update
  - **Success Metric**: Q&A engagement and follow-up requests
  - **Timeline**: Week 6-8

#### External Visibility Goals
- **Goal**: Establish industry presence
  - **Action**: Submit speaking proposal to relevant conference
  - **Success Metric**: Acceptance or invitation to participate
  - **Timeline**: Submit by day 45

### Days 61-90: Strategic Impact

#### Business Impact Goals
- **Goal**: Demonstrate measurable business value
  - **Action**: Complete cost optimization or revenue initiative
  - **Success Metric**: $1M+ impact identified or achieved
  - **Timeline**: Results by day 90

- **Goal**: Influence strategic decision
  - **Action**: Provide technology perspective on major business decision
  - **Success Metric**: Recommendation accepted and implemented
  - **Timeline**: Ongoing, achieve by day 90

## Long-Term Development Goals (6-12 Months)

### Q1-Q2: Building CTO Capabilities

#### Strategic Leadership
**Goal**: Develop and socialize 3-year technology strategy
- **Milestones**:
  - Month 1: Research and benchmarking
  - Month 2: Draft strategy and stakeholder input
  - Month 3: Executive and board approval
- **Success Metrics**: 
  - Board approval of strategy
  - Budget allocation aligned to strategy
  - Organization rally around vision

#### Scale Leadership
**Goal**: Expand leadership span from 25 to 100+ people
- **Milestones**:
  - Month 1-2: Restructure organization for scale
  - Month 3-4: Develop/hire senior leaders
  - Month 5-6: Implement governance and communication
- **Success Metrics**:
  - Employee engagement maintained or improved
  - Delivery velocity increased
  - Leadership team NPS > 4.0

### Q3-Q4: CTO Readiness Demonstration

#### Executive Presence
**Goal**: Establish recognized executive presence
- **Milestones**:
  - Speak at 2 industry conferences
  - Publish 3 thought leadership articles
  - Regular board/committee participation
- **Success Metrics**:
  - External speaking invitations increase
  - Media mentions and quotes
  - Board comfort with technology discussions

#### Business Partnership
**Goal**: Become trusted business partner beyond technology
- **Milestones**:
  - Co-lead major business initiative
  - Influence product/market strategy
  - Drive P&L improvement through technology
- **Success Metrics**:
  - Invited to non-technology strategic discussions
  - Business leaders seek technology input proactively
  - Measurable revenue or margin impact

## SMART Goal Templates

### Template 1: Strategic Initiative Leadership
**Specific**: Lead [initiative name] affecting [# of employees/customers]
**Measurable**: Deliver [specific outcomes] with [metrics]
**Achievable**: Leverage [resources/support] with [timeline]
**Relevant**: Aligns with [company priority] and demonstrates [CTO capability]
**Time-bound**: Complete by [date] with milestones at [intervals]

**Example filled out**:
- **S**: Lead customer experience digital transformation affecting 1M customers
- **M**: Improve NPS by 10 points and reduce service costs by 20%
- **A**: $5M budget, 20-person team, executive sponsorship
- **R**: Top company priority, demonstrates enterprise leadership
- **T**: 9-month program with quarterly milestones

### Template 2: Capability Development
**Specific**: Develop [specific capability] through [method]
**Measurable**: Demonstrate competency via [assessment/feedback]
**Achievable**: Dedicate [time/resources] with [support]
**Relevant**: Critical for CTO role because [reason]
**Time-bound**: Achieve proficiency by [date]

**Example filled out**:
- **S**: Develop board presentation skills through coaching and practice
- **M**: Successfully present to board with 4.5/5 feedback score
- **A**: Monthly coaching sessions, quarterly board committee attendance
- **R**: CTOs present to board quarterly on strategy and risk
- **T**: First presentation in 3 months, proficiency in 6 months

## Stretch Assignment Ideas

### For Strategic Leadership Development
1. **Lead Strategic Planning Process**: Own technology input to company strategic plan
2. **Board Presentation**: Present technology strategy to board committee
3. **M&A Due Diligence**: Lead technology evaluation for acquisition
4. **Digital Transformation**: Drive enterprise-wide transformation initiative

### For Executive Presence Development
1. **Industry Conference Keynote**: Deliver keynote at major industry event
2. **Media Spokesperson**: Represent company in media interviews
3. **Executive Committee Membership**: Join executive committee as technology voice
4. **Customer Executive Briefings**: Lead quarterly briefings for key customers

### For Business Acumen Development
1. **P&L Ownership**: Take P&L responsibility for technology products
2. **Budget Planning**: Partner with CFO on annual technology budget
3. **Business Case Development**: Create and defend major investment case
4. **Revenue Generation**: Launch technology-enabled revenue stream

### For Scale Leadership Development
1. **Organizational Merger**: Integrate two technology organizations
2. **Transformation Leadership**: Lead 100+ person transformation
3. **Global Team Management**: Build and manage distributed global team
4. **Succession Planning**: Develop comprehensive succession plan
